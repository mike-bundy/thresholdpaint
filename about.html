Our values

How we work

Prep before paint

The finish is only as good as what's under it. We wash, scrape, sand, prime and caulk before a drop of color goes on. Knowing when to stop prepping is the hardest part of the job, and the part that separates a two-year paint job from a fifteen-year one.

Respect for the home

Historic craftsman, mid-century ranch or new build, we treat each house like it's ours. Furniture protected, floors covered, lead-safe practices on older homes, and a site left cleaner than we found it.

Straight communication

Prompt callbacks, itemized estimates, realistic timelines, and updates as the work moves. You'll always know what's happening and what comes next.
