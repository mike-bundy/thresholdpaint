Exterior Painting

Oregon weather is hard on a house. Our exterior repaints start with a full wash and careful prep so the finish bonds and lasts. We use premium 100% acrylic coatings suited to the Pacific Northwest, and we back-brush where it counts.

  • Pressure washing & surface prep
  • Scraping, sanding & priming
  • Caulking & minor wood repair
  • Two finish coats, brush & spray
  • Lead-safe practices for pre-1978 homes
  • Siding, trim, doors, garages, fences

Interior Painting

Interior work is about crisp lines and a clean job site. We mask and protect everything, cut in by hand, and leave each room ready to live in at the end of the day. Ask us about the 1:4 ceiling trick from the Paint Initiative journal.

  • Walls, ceilings & trim
  • Accent walls & feature colors
  • Doors, windows & millwork
  • Color consultation & sampling
  • Low-VOC and scuff-resistant products
  • Furniture moved, floors protected

Cabinets & Trim

Painting cabinets is the highest-impact upgrade in a kitchen and the least forgiving to do wrong. We follow full cure times between coats and spray cabinet-specific enamels for a factory-smooth finish that resists chipping. You'll still be able to cook dinner each night.

  • Kitchen & bath cabinet refinishing
  • Degrease, sand, prime, spray
  • Cabinet-grade enamels for durability
  • Two-tone and island color changes
  • Built-ins, shelving & mantels
  • Baseboard, casing & crown
Get an estimate

Wood Finishing & Decks

Wood needs care to survive our winters. We strip failed finishes, bring the grain back with proper sanding, and apply penetrating stains or sealers that protect from moisture and UV. Whether you want the cedar to glow or a solid color that matches the house, we'll get you there.

  • Deck cleaning, sanding & staining
  • Fence & gate staining
  • Cedar siding & porch railings
  • Solid, semi-transparent & clear finishes
  • Exterior stairs & landings
  • Annual maintenance plans
Get an estimate

Drywall Repair & Texture

Great paint can't hide bad drywall. We repair the damage, match the existing texture so the patch disappears, and prime before painting. From a doorknob hole to a ceiling that's been leaking for a year, we've fixed it.

  • Water damage & ceiling repair
  • Holes, cracks & nail pops
  • Texture matching: orange peel, knockdown, smooth
  • Skim coating
  • Pipe & fixture penetrations
  • Paint-ready finish
Get an estimate

Maintenance & Power Washing

Little things keep a house looking loved. A yearly wash, a few touch-ups and fresh caulk can add years to a paint job. We offer maintenance visits so you never have to think about it.

  • Power washing: siding, decks, walks
  • Seasonal touch-ups
  • Peeling & failure spot repair
  • Caulk & seal maintenance
  • Small carpentry fixes
  • Pre-sale refresh packages
